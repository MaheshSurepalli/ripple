Privacy Policy for Ripple

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Ripple ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you participate in social challenge chains.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Display name, username, and optional profile avatar.
  • Challenge Responses: Photos and text messages you submit as responses to challenges. Your response is kept private and only revealed to your direct inviter and friends you pass the challenge forward to after they submit their response.
  • Approximate Location: When enabled, coarse city and country names (e.g. "Denver, United States") to display the spread of a Ripple. We never record or store precise GPS coordinates.
  • Device Permissions: Camera access is used solely for capturing live photo responses within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To reconstruct the Ripple propagation tree and display generation depth.
  • To calculate aggregate viral statistics (e.g. total people reached, distance traveled).
  • To enforce content moderation, safety rules, and prevent spam.

3. Data Sharing & Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Responses are end-to-end access-controlled via Cloud Security Rules.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@ripple.app.
