Privacy Policy for Ripple

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Welcome to Ripple ("we", "our", or "us"). Ripple is a viral social challenge platform where participants complete challenges to unlock friends' responses and pass them forward.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your data when you use the Ripple Android mobile application and associated online services, in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Display name, chosen username, and optional profile avatar URL.
  • Challenge Submissions: Photo and text responses submitted as part of challenge participation. Responses are stored in encrypted cloud storage and are protected by server security rules.
  • Approximate Location (Optional): If you grant location permission, we record only coarse city and country names (such as "Denver, United States") to display the spread of the Ripple chain on the map. We never record, track, or store precise GPS coordinates or real-time location.
  • Device Camera & Media: Camera access is used exclusively when you capture a photo response to a challenge. Photos are not accessed without your direct interaction.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage telemetry (via Firebase Analytics) to monitor server health, crash rates, and tree propagation performance.

2. Reveal Privacy & Chain Isolation

A core design principle of Ripple is Reveal Privacy:

  • When someone invites you to a challenge, their photo/text response is locked and inaccessible on your device until you submit your own response.
  • Cloud Security Rules enforce that other participants' private submissions cannot be queried or downloaded until your submission is atomically verified by the server.

3. How We Use Information

  • To reconstruct the Ripple propagation tree, track generation depth, and calculate distance traveled.
  • To prevent duplicate submissions, cheaters, and chain spam via cryptographic invite tokens.
  • To enforce safety guidelines and process user-submitted content reports.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or user-generated content to advertisers or third-party data brokers. Data is processed solely through trusted infrastructure providers (Google Cloud / Firebase).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account, submissions, and profile data at any time. To request data deletion, contact us at privacy@ripple.app or via our GitHub repository.

6. Age Requirements

Ripple is intended for users aged 18 and older (18–35 demographic). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us:
